Version 0.4.21 of the LEGO Battles PC port: joining a lobby that sends you custom units or maps no longer drops you at the title screen, armies stop turning into King's for no visible reason, and a batch of player suggestions.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Back in the lobby after loading the host's content.** When a host's custom units or map arrive, the game still resets to load them, but it now walks back to the same lobby and joins it by itself. Setting: Multiplayer > "Rejoin after loading the host's content" (on).
- **Your army fits the host's rules in the lobby.** If the host places a creature in a different slot (for example the Wolf as Close Combat while your saved army has it as Mounted), or has creatures off, that one slot becomes King's unit for it as soon as you join, and the lobby tells you. Your own army comes back when you leave the lobby. A unit the host has banned is named in the same notice, so you can change it before the match.
- **Area Heal** can be given to heroes in Balance > Heroes and in the Unit Lab, and appears in the hero's spell brick (the original game hides it).
- **Smarter Unit Movement** (Balance > Spells, off by default, the host's rule online): units ordered as a group each get their own destination tile instead of all heading for the same one.
- **Per-unit repairing**: a "Repairs" trait in Balance > Traits, the Unit Lab and unit.json (`can_repair`), for foot, ranged and mounted units. A selection without a builder can now be ordered to repair.
- **Dragon fire breath for custom units** on a dragon's model: unit.json `"attack_anim": "breath"`, or the Unit Lab checkbox (off by default).
- **"Never in Random Armies" lists custom units** too, and the Randomizer respects them.
- The Unit Lab's **sharing name** is kept between launches.

## Fixes

- **Whole army switched to King's in online matches** when the saved army had a creature in a slot the host uses differently. Only that slot changes now. When a real ban does switch an army, the game says why on screen.
- **Rejoining a lobby with the fast online link** ended in "Your connection has been lost": the host took the returning player for the old connection.
- **Couldn't ready up** as a joiner with custom units when the host had none: the game reset again and again.
- **Custom units with their own 3D texture** kept their stock team colour under a custom team colour.
- **Specials invisible** in big three-army matches (the teal team's especially): there is room for more 3D models at once (with "Larger Sprite Pool").
- **Hotwired special units** could be drawn from a missing model record.
- **"Show enemies" and "Reveal map"** showed up for you even when the red brick cheats were set to help the computer only.

## Still open

- **Specials invisible when zoomed out** (Enhanced), **phantom ties at the very start of an online match**, **cutscene audio stutter** and the **sprite editor** request: we asked the reporters for details.
- **EMP on a hotwired vehicle** froze an online match in older versions. Not reproduced; please send the session log if it happens.
- **Computer players in online matches**: held back; not ready yet.
- **Scripting and custom spells**: planned as a later project.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby should use the same version.
