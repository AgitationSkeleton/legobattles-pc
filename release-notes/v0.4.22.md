Version 0.4.22 of the LEGO Battles PC port: units no longer vanish on wide custom maps, the screen-swap button no longer locks up clicks, and a fixed window size, custom names and two new optional rules.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Fixed window size** (Settings > Display, off by default): presets from 768x576 (DS x3) to 3840x2160, or your own width and height. The window keeps that size and can't be resized while it is on.
- **Custom names** (Enhancements > Renames > Custom Names): give any unit or building a name of your own. Only you see it; other players, Discord and the servers keep the game's names.
- **Faster Pathfinding** (Balance > Spells, off by default, the host's rule online): units stop at a mountain, trees or water for seconds before going round because the game's route planner gets very little work each moment, shared by every unit. With this on it finds the way round at once. In a test, twelve units crossing a long mountain wall arrived about a third sooner.
- **Fix Thunder Hammer** (Balance > Spells, off by default, the host's rule online): in the original game a Thunder Hammer thrown at a unit fizzles when it reaches it, because its hit check compares the target's team with itself. With this on, it explodes on the unit with its own blast, as a hammer thrown at the ground does.
- **Team Builder custom colour**: a colour wheel with RGB and hex fields, in plain view (it used to sit behind the swatch).

## Fixes

- **Units disappeared past a certain distance on non-square maps** (for example 128x64). The original game limits the area it draws to the map's height across and its width down; every stock map is square, so it never showed. Fixed in both DS Vanilla and Enhanced.
- **Clicks stopped working mid-match** after pressing Select (Backspace or a controller's Select). That is the game's screen-swap button; in Enhanced it moved touch to the hidden info screen. Select now does nothing in Enhanced and still swaps in DS Vanilla.
- **"Hide enemy specials in fog" hid every special while Reveal Map was on.**

## Still open

- **Story-only buildings as level-editor decor**: not yet. A map can only place buildings that an army in the match fields, and these buildings' art isn't loaded outside their story missions.
- **Specials invisible when zoomed out** (Enhanced), **phantom ties at the very start of an online match**, **cutscene audio stutter** and the **sprite editor** request: we asked the reporters for details.
- **Computer players in online matches**: held back; not ready yet.
- **Scripting and custom spells**: planned as a later project.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby should use the same version. The two new rules follow the host's settings.
