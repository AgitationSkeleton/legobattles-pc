Version 0.4.28 of the LEGO Battles PC port: Visuals 2.0, steadier battles, online play through HTTPS, and new options.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Visuals 2.0, by Jaime A. Vives

Settings > Display > **Visuals**: Visuals 1.0 (the look you know, still the default) or **Visuals 2.0**, ported from Jaime A. Vives' LEGO Battles PC project with his permission. Every part also has its own switch.
- **Story films**: cleaned and doubled in resolution (Anime4K), shown in a letterboxed theatre.
- **Presentation**: victory fireworks and a screen shake for big spells.
- **A PC HUD**: a resource bar, a minimap panel (click it to move the camera) and a unit card in your team's colour, including multi-unit selections and building queues.
- **Sprite effects** from the game's own art.
- **His controls**, as bindable shortcuts (Input > "Jaime A. Vives' controls"). Nothing is bound unless you choose.

## Smoother battles

- **Battles no longer stutter at 30 Hz.** Each new picture of the battlefield used to arrive one or three frames after the last, depending on how busy the game was, which looked like dropped frames. Every picture now stays exactly two frames, with steady 30 Hz pacing on (the default).
- **Faster CPU** now sizes its speed for the heaviest recent moment instead of creeping down until the game fell behind.

## Online

- **Joining failed when a direct connection wasn't possible.** The relay now goes over HTTPS, like the rest of the game's online traffic.
- **A crashed host's lobby stays listed for less time** (about 40 s instead of 90), and the lobby list in the Esc menu now refreshes while it is open, so a lobby that's gone disappears there too.
- **Tournament Rules** (Network > Multiplayer): one switch that puts every option that changes the game at its default, here and in lobbies you host, without losing your own choices. Bans, maps and performance options are untouched.
- **Community announcements** (Network > Multiplayer): news from the server's moderators, such as upcoming tournaments. A new one shows a notice once.

## Fixes

- **The HUD scale could push the blue brick out of the window.** Scaled HUD elements now stay inside it.
- **Unit icons in the build lists and queues** take your team's colour, and queue icons are centred.
- **The fog lingered at the start of a battle** (and under Reveal Map) until the camera moved.
- **The computer built shipyards even when its army had nothing to train there.**

## New options (off by default)

- **Attack Move** (Settings > Controls): an Attack order tapped on the ground near an enemy attacks the nearest enemy you can see.
- **The AI Clears Trees To Gold Bricks** (Enhancements): computer units cut through the trees in their way to a Gold Brick.
- **Balance > "Export spells and Gold Brick as cheat codes..."**: your spell and Gold Brick values as Action Replay codes, for a DS cheat device, a flashcart or an emulator (an Entities.ebp can't hold them).

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby should use the same version. Players on 0.4.27 or older can't use the relay, because the server now requires HTTPS.
