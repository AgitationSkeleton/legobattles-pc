Version 0.3.3 of the LEGO Battles PC port: a second computer opponent, Ninjago's Free Play maps, more for the Balance editor, and a backup online server.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **A second computer opponent in Free Play.** Team Builder > **Computer Player 3** adds a third army to a Free Play battle on a map with room for it.
  - It has its own army (a faction, your Team Builder's enemy team, or Randomizer), its own colour, and can be **allied with the other computer**.
  - You win by destroying every opponent.
  - Offline only.
- **Ninjago's Free Play maps.** The 22 Battle Mode maps of LEGO Battles: Ninjago, converted, under their own names: Iceberg (Ninjago), Asunder (Ninjago), The Middle (Ninjago) and so on.
  - Trees stand where Ninjago's brick piles are, and every base has wood in reach.
  - Get them from the **Maps** page (the "LEGO Battles PC custom maps" pack).
- **Two-player maps are listed.** Free Play used to show only maps with three or more starts; maps with two now appear too (the Ninjago maps, and others).
- **Up to 96 custom maps** load at once (it was 30).
- **Projectile sprites** (Balance > Attacks). Each projectile has a **Sprite** choice, including the game's unused Ninja Star, Flaming Cannonball and Large Arrow. Four unused projectile records are offered as **Spare projectile 1-4**, so a unit can have a projectile of its own.
- **Builder yield** (Balance). A builder's **Yield** sets the bricks it gets per tree chopped. 0 keeps the game's 75; the Mill bonus and Double Bricks still apply.
- **Neutral creatures in the Mounted slot.** The Wolf, Robot and Monkey can now stand in Close Combat, Ranged or Mounted.

## Maps page

- **It lists everything in both map archives,** read from GitHub each time, so new packs show up without a game update. A pack that changes in its archive is downloaded again.
- **The grid wraps** to the window's width.
- **Two community maps that were missing now show:** Supply Lines 1, and 29-Better Dead Marsh, which uses the stock Dead Marsh minimap.

## Fixed

- **The "Random" map's question mark** is a clean, tilted "?" with a bevel and a shadow, instead of a ragged copy of the Random opponent's icon.
- **Banning every map** no longer leaves an empty map list: the bans are set aside for that list, and a notice says so.
- **Random maps** can now draw two-player maps.

## Online

- **A backup server.** If the main online server answers with a Cloudflare limit error, or can't be reached, the game switches to a backup server by itself. It switches back once the main server answers and you are not in a lobby. Lobbies on the backup are its own.
- **Fewer requests.** An open lobby checks for joining players every 2 seconds for its first 2 minutes, then every 6.
