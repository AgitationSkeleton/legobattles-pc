Version 0.3.8 of the LEGO Battles PC port: the game's unused teal team as a seventh team colour, a working team tint for the Shark, and the game's own unit names outside the game.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Teal team colour** (Team Builder, off by default). The cartridge has a seventh team colour it never offers; this adds it to the colour screens: Free Play, the multiplayer faction screen and the lobbies, and as a choice for Computer Player 3. Teal units, buildings and minimap dots get their own colour. Online, the host's setting applies to everyone in the lobby.
- **How teal looks**, beside the option: **White/Teal (Original)** (the default) is the cartridge's own unused teal, which draws mostly white with pale teal and teal accents. **Teal (PC Port)** is a full teal ramp like the other teams'. This only changes how teal looks on your screen, so two players in the same match can pick differently. A change shows from the next level.

## Fixed

- **Tint creatures by team** now works for the Shark: an owned Shark's body takes its owner's team colour. Before, the option had no effect on it.
- **Rich Presence, the match picture, the Discord report and the lobby servers** use the game's own unit and building names, such as "Space Criminal Leader". Names you set under Enhancements > Renames still show in the game itself.

## Files

- `LegoBattles-PC-0.3.8-runner-source.zip`: the corresponding source of the runner (GPL-3.0-or-later). The port's own source is this repository at this tag.
