Version 0.4.0 of the LEGO Battles PC port: a level editor, and a community library to share levels in.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Level Editor** (Maps > Level Editor). Make your own Free Play levels, or open any Free Play or story map from your cartridge to study or remake it.
  - **Terrain:** paint grass, stone, water, mountains and trees with a brush, line, rectangle, ellipse or fill, and pick a terrain with the eyedropper. Shores, cliffs and edges join up by themselves, drawn with the game's own tiles. Switch a level between the King, Pirate and Mars worlds.
  - **Start bases:** place up to four, each with a preview of its base, barracks, farm, hero and builders.
  - **Other objects:** mines, quarries and well caps; bridge sites, checked as 3, 6 or 9 water cells across (or carved to fit); AI markers, by hand or automatically; golden bricks; and buildings and units for any player.
  - **Editing:** select, copy, cut and paste with a preview, then delete. Undo and redo work with Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y, and the other shortcuts are Ctrl+C / X / V / A / S, Delete and Esc.
  - **Checks:** a Checks list warns about starts on water, unreachable starts, bad bridge sites and missing markers.
  - **Save and play:** Save puts the level in your mods/maps folder. **Load new maps now** adds it to Free Play and multiplayer, and lobbies send it to players who don't have it. Export writes an `.lbz` package, and Open reads `.lbz` and `.map` files.
- **Community levels** (Maps > Community levels). Browse levels other players have shared, see a preview, and download one to play, or open it in the editor to remix it.
  - **Share yours:** use Level Editor > Share online. A moderator reviews each upload before it appears.
  - **Checks on the server:** an upload must be a complete, valid level and is checked byte for byte. Level and author names are checked too.
  - **Checks on your PC:** a downloaded level is checked again before it is saved.

## Changed

- **Large uploads** to the master server are now always sent in full.
