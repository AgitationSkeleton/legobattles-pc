Version 0.4.10 of the LEGO Battles PC port: phantom ties, and "always affected" spell options.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Online

- **New: Prevent Phantom Ties** (Network > Multiplayer, on by default; the host's choice applies to its lobby). A phantom tie is when the two games of a match drift apart. One cause is now measured: ESP and EMP stun length (Super Magic) and spell range (the range cheat) follow each machine's own cheats. Two players with different cheats therefore see different fights. With the setting on, everyone plays with the host's cheats; this is what the port already did, now as a setting. Off plays each machine's own cheats, as on a DS. Tested with two games: off, a different unit died on each screen; on, every freeze matched to the frame.
- **Please update together:** custom units with the new "Always" options (below) read as "Immune" in 0.4.9.

## Custom units and balance

- **New: Always hotwireable, EMP-able and ESP-able.** The Hotwire, EMP and ESP options (Unit Lab Stats tab, and Balance > Traits for stock units) are now **Normal / Immune / Always**. Always lets the spell take the unit whatever kind it is: hotwire a foot soldier, EMP a cavalry unit, ESP a ship.
- **Changed:** "EMP Disables Transports" now works through the same code path. It plays the same as before.

## Still open

- **EMP on a hotwired vehicle froze a match** (three reports from one match). We staged it online, with a stolen special and a stolen transport and the same host settings, and could not reproduce it. If it happens to you, please post the session log from the logs folder.
