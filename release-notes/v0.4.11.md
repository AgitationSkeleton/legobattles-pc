Version 0.4.11 of the LEGO Battles PC port: invisible units, walls in Enhanced, a crash on Save, mountains for custom units, and level editor additions.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Fixes

- **Invisible, unkillable units.** A custom unit (or a Balance edit) with **Building collision** turned off stood in none of the game's occupancy layers, so it could not be seen, clicked or attacked. It could still move and shoot, and only select-all picked it. Such a unit now keeps the ground layer and behaves normally. Tested with an affected custom unit from a report.
- **Walls and bridges popping in and out in Enhanced.** Since 0.4.8, walls and built bridges were drawn only inside the DS's own 256 columns. In the wider Enhanced view they appeared and vanished as the camera moved, worst when zoomed out. They now draw across the whole view. DS Vanilla was never affected.
- **Crash when saving Options in a battle.** Pause > Options > Save crashed the game on the next frame, whether or not the volume was changed. The game reloaded the menu fonts mid-battle. In a battle that reload is now left for the next time a profile loads. Your volume and language are still saved.
- **No clicks after switching Enhanced to DS Vanilla mid-battle.** Every tap went to the last Enhanced click. Switching display mode during a battle now works in both directions.
- **Mountains tag on custom units.** Units with "Can walk over mountains" stopped at the cliff edge. They now cross. Units without the tag are still stopped by cliffs.

## New

- **More than 10 mine spots per map** (Balance, beside EMP Disables Transports; on by default; online, the host's setting applies). Maps can have up to 32 mine, quarry or well cap spots. The game's limit was 10, and spots past the tenth did nothing. Stock maps have 10 or fewer and play the same either way.
- **Mouse buttons for battle shortcuts** (Settings > Controls > Shortcut keys). Every shortcut can also be bound to middle click, right click, Mouse 4 or Mouse 5. For example, you can swap which back/forward button opens the blue menu. The defaults are unchanged.
- **Level editor:**
  - Bridge sites now get the bridge posts you see on stock maps.
  - **Gravel** is a new terrain brush: the darker patches on stone that every world's tileset has.
  - The decorative tile picker has a zoom slider, scrolls sideways, can be made taller, and shows a large preview of the tile under the cursor.
  - The checks note maps with more than 10 mine spots.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby needs 0.4.11. The new mine-spot rule and the Always options from 0.4.10 are read differently by older versions.

## Still open

- **EMP on a hotwired vehicle froze a match** (reports from one match on 0.4.9). It could not be reproduced. 0.4.10 fixed one cause of matches drifting apart (Prevent Phantom Ties). If it happens again, please post the session log from the logs folder.
- **The computer player building shipyards on maps that don't need transports** is analysed and planned for a later version.
