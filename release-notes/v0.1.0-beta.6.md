The sixth test build of the LEGO Battles PC port. **This is an untested pre-release: nothing new in it has been tested in real play yet.** Each change was only checked in automated runs on one PC, with online play tested between two copies on that PC, including simulated internet delay and packet loss. None has been tried by players or between two PCs. Expect rough edges, and please report what you find on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legobattles-pc/issues/new/choose).

**Everyone in an online match needs this version.** Its online link cannot talk to beta.5 or earlier.

## What's new (untested in real play)

- **Smooth online play.**
  - The DS wireless protocol waited for a full network round trip on every game step, so an online match crawled: about 16 steps a second at a typical internet delay, where the game runs 30.
  - The new "Fast online link" (Multiplayer section, on by default) buffers a few steps instead, so the match keeps its full speed. The cost is a small input delay that grows with the connection's delay: about 0.1 s nearby, 0.3 s at a 230 ms round trip.
  - In automated tests it held 29–30 steps a second, in sync, with up to 230 ms of round-trip delay and 5% packet loss.
  - Every player in a match needs the same setting.
- **A relay when no direct connection is possible.**
  - Some networks let no game traffic in at all: a VPN, carrier-grade NAT, a strict firewall.
  - When a joiner's direct connection carries nothing for five seconds, the game now connects both players through the lobby server `lbserver.viosarcade.xyz` instead. Both players dial out, so neither needs an open port.
  - The Multiplayer section shows "Relay: …" while it is in use.
- **Both lobby servers by default.** Hosting lists your lobby on `lbserver.viosarcade.xyz` and `king.legobattles.win`, and the Join screen shows the lobbies of both. If you had picked `king.legobattles.win` alone or a custom server, that choice is kept; any server you pick from now on is kept too.

## Known limitations

- **Testing so far.** Everything above has only had automated checks on one PC. The fast link and the relay have not been used between two different PCs.
- **The relay is for players who need it.** It adds the trip to the server and back, and it only carries lobbies listed on `lbserver.viosarcade.xyz` (with Both, every lobby is).
- **Still not seen in a real battle** (built, off by default): EMP on transports, hotwired transports leaving their old owner, and the fix for a freeze when a hotwired transport's occupants are destroyed.

## Reporting

Attach the newest file in `logs\` beside the executable and, if you can, the newest `lb-session-*.lbdemo`. For online problems, the lines starting `[lb_vwh]` and `[lb_relay]` in the log say what the link was doing.
