Version 0.4.13 of the LEGO Battles PC port: a crash with mountain-crossing custom units, and team colours on the creature portraits.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Fixes

- **Crash in long matches with a mountain-crossing custom unit loaded.** Since 0.4.11, when any loaded custom unit had "Can walk over mountains", every unit's route search treated land beyond cliffs as reachable. The game then searched whole maps for routes nobody could walk, ran out of memory for its searches, and crashed, typically a few minutes into a match with several armies. Now only the tagged units ignore cliffs, per order. Every other unit plans routes as in the original game. Tested with three computer armies on Avast for eight minutes with the AI shipyards setting on, as in the report: no crash. The tagged unit still crosses mountains.

## New

- **Team colours on the creature portraits.** The Monkey, Wolf, Shark and Robot portraits on the Top Screen and in the online team picture now show their team colour, as the creatures themselves do: the Monkey's brown fur, the Robot's orange body and neck, and the grey of the Wolf and Shark.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby needs 0.4.13 when a custom unit with "Can walk over mountains" is loaded. Older versions plan routes differently and the match would drift apart.

## Still open

- **EMP on a hotwired vehicle froze a match** (reports from one match on 0.4.8). It could not be reproduced. The game logs where a bad memory read comes from, so if it happens again, please post the session log from the logs folder.
- **Editing spells** (damage, timing, duration, mana cost, range and area) is planned for a later version.
