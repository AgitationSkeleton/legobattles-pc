Version 0.4.2 of the LEGO Battles PC port adds custom units: make your own units and buildings from the game's own, play them in Free Play and online, and share them with other players.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Custom units** (Balance > Custom units).
  - **Preview** any unit or building animated, in every direction, action and team colour. **Compare** shows a second one beside it.
  - **Export** a unit as a template: its stats (`unit.json`), sprite sheets and frames as PNGs, cards, head, HUD icon, model texture and sounds as WAVs.
  - **Make a custom unit** from an export, then edit its pictures, sounds and stats. Sheets can also be NCBR files, such as community sprite sheets.
  - **Custom units are added, never swapped in.** Each one is a new unit beside the stock ones. Tick it and press **Load new units now**. It then appears in the Team Builder and in the game's own army screen, with its own card, in Free Play and in lobbies.
  - **Its own sounds.** In battle, a custom unit plays the WAVs in its `sounds` folder for select, death, strike, hit and special.
  - **Online.** A host's custom units are sent to everyone who joins the lobby. Their game reloads with the same units before they can ready up.
- **Community units** (Balance > Custom units > Community units).
  - **Share online** uploads one of your units. Like levels, it appears once the moderator approves it.
  - **Browse** by kind (hero, builder, mounted, barracks and so on) and sort by newest, oldest, most downloaded or most liked.
  - **Subscribe** to add a unit to your game. **Unsubscribe** removes it again. **Like** the ones you enjoy.

## Changed

- **Settings menu text.** Labels and tooltips are shorter and plainer.

## Fixed

- **"Resume" was cut short.** On the objective and red-brick screens opened during a battle, the Resume button read "Resum" or lost its text.
