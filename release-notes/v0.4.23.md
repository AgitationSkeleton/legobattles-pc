Version 0.4.23 of the LEGO Battles PC port: play a map straight from the level editor, and mounted units animate correctly in the Custom units viewer.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Play this map** (Level editor > Level): saves the level, reloads the map lists and starts a Free Play battle on it by itself, with your profile, army and opponent settings. It ends any match in progress.

## Fixes

- **Mounted units' walk showed a frame of their attack** in Balance > Custom units, and custom mounts built from frame pictures had it too. Mounts have 3 walk frames and 5 attack frames (the walk was taken as 4 and 4). Unit folders exported by earlier versions still build as they were drawn.

## Still open

- **Story-only buildings as level-editor decor**: not yet. A map can only place buildings that an army in the match fields, and these buildings' art isn't loaded outside their story missions.
- **Specials invisible when zoomed out** (Enhanced), **phantom ties at the very start of an online match**, **cutscene audio stutter** and the **sprite editor** request: we asked the reporters for details.
- **Computer players in online matches**: held back; not ready yet.
- **Scripting and custom spells**: planned as a later project.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby should use the same version.
