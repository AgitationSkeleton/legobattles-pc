Version 0.4.26 of the LEGO Battles PC port: online play moves to Discord sign-in.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Discord sign-in for online play

As the game moves toward a more public stage, online play now requires signing in with a Discord account. You need a sign-in to connect to the master servers, and to publish custom maps, custom units or high scores.

- **Network > Account > Sign in with Discord** opens Discord in your browser. Approve LEGO Battles PC there, then come back: the game finishes signing in by itself. No username or password is needed. The game only learns your Discord name and id.
- The same page lists the maps and units you publish.

## Self-hosting: no account needed

Self-hosting is still an option, and it doesn't need a Discord account.

- **Direct IP play** (Network > Multiplayer > *Direct IP play*) connects players by address for ad-hoc games, with no master server. The host opens the game's Host screen and shares their address and the UDP port shown; the others connect to it, then join from the game's Join screen.
- **Your own master server**: enter it as a Custom master server.

How to run one is in the repository's README under *Playing without a Discord account (self-hosting)*. Self-hosted games and servers don't connect to the official master servers or their channels.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby should use the same version.
