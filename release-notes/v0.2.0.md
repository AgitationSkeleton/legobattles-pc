The LEGO Battles PC port, version 0.2.0. This is the first full release: the port has been played widely, online and offline, so builds are no longer marked as test pre-releases. It covers most of the bug reports and suggestions collected in the Discord. Please keep reporting in the Discord bug-report and suggestion channels or on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legobattles-pc/issues/new/choose).

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc.

## What's new

### Ban maps, random maps and ban presets

- **Ban Maps** (Balance > Ban Maps): ticked maps leave the Free Play and host map lists. Mod maps can be banned too.
- **Per-map unit bans:** on Ban Units, right-click a unit to ban it on some maps only. A table under the grid lists them.
- **Random map:** tick **Add Random to the map lists**, and **Random** is first in the Free Play and host map lists. The preview and the lobby show the map it drew.
- **Presets:** a preset sets the unit bans and the map bans together. The game includes the LEGO Battles Discord competitive rules (2026-10). **Export** and **Import** share your own as a `.json` file.
- **Tournament picks:** while a preset is in force, **Random (tourney)** draws only from that preset's allowed maps, and **Random team (tourney)** gives every player a random army with no banned unit. Every player gets the same draw.
- **Online, the host's map rules apply to everyone** in its lobby, like its unit bans.

### Online fixes

- **The host's other settings now apply to everyone**, so these no longer cause a desync:
  - the hotwire options;
  - the transport options;
  - the ranged attack boost;
  - the hotwired-healing fix;
  - cheats;
  - the unit and building caps.

  The pages show the host's values while you are in its lobby.
- **Version warning:** when you join a host that runs a different version, the game says so, because the match may go out of sync.
- **Joining a lobby no longer shows the previous game's map.**
- **Fixed a crash** joining a lobby on a custom map after a game Reset.

### The Discord log and Discord status

- **The match picture** now uses the map's real Free Play colours and each player's real team colour.
- **The lobby log says who won** when a match ends.
- **Discord status:** Discord shows the map you're playing, your hero and the match time. Turn it off with **Discord status** in Settings > General; **Show my hero in Discord status** hides only the hero.
- **Check for updates** (Settings > General) shows the newest release and links to it. **Check for updates at startup** does it at launch.

### The computer opponent

- **More than one AI hero:** with the hero cap raised and **The AI Rebuilds Its Hero Every Time** on, the AI now fields heroes up to the cap. Before, it built only one. Offline only.
- **AI Uses Targeted Spells** (Balance > Spells): the computer's heroes also cast the spells aimed at one unit, such as Hotwire. In the original game the AI only casts spells on the ground or on itself. Off by default, and offline only.

### Display and controls

- **Zoom no longer hides the map edge:** at some zoom levels the camera could not reach the outer columns of large maps, such as Outbreak's bottom left. On large windows, the lowest Magnification values now act as the smallest zoom that still shows the whole map.
- **Reset no longer zooms the map to full size** in the next battle.
- **Forced tilesets:** small islands and mounds no longer draw a shoreline piece in their middle.
- **DS Vanilla layout:**
  - mouse clicks now land where you point;
  - the two screens have their own size sliders, with typed values; **Reset screen sizes** restores them;
  - swapping the screens works in this layout.
- **Swap screens:** a key (Settings > Input > **Swap screens key**) and a **Swap screens** button.
- **A key bound to Tab now works.**
- **Skip the intro screens** (Settings > Display): go straight to the title screen. Off by default.

### Menus and names

- **Balance holds every balance option:** Ban Units, Unit Caps and the new Spells page, which has the hotwire options, now sit under Balance.
- **Six more renames** (Enhancements > Renames, all off by default):
  - Alien Assault Ship → Dragon Cruiser;
  - Alien Attack Ship → Crystal Extractor;
  - Earth Transport Ship → Recon Dropship;
  - Alien Transport Ship → Hyper Carrier;
  - Starfighter → Switch Fighter;
  - Starship 1 → Rocket.

## Known limitations

- **Minikit specials under balance presets:** a report says their range stays at the vanilla value. The preset's values are in the game, so if you see this, please send the session recording (`lb-session-*.lbdemo`).
- **The mount picture bug** (a builder's mount shows the wrong picture) is not fixed yet.
- **Neutral units in the army builder** (locked King card, missing icons) are not fixed yet.
- **A Random map can land on a mod map a joiner lacks.** That joiner then cannot ready, as with any mod map.
- **Three-player lobbies** have not been tried with the map bans.
