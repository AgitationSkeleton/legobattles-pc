Version 0.3.1 of the LEGO Battles PC port: fixes for 0.3.0, the map catalogue, and the game updating itself from now on.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc.

## Fixed

- **The Maps page listed nothing.** The 0.3.0 download unpacked no zip, so the browser showed no maps and a lobby could not fetch a map from the archive. Both work now.
- **Custom maps placed straight in `mods/`** are now found. Before, only `mods/maps/` was read; any folder under `mods/` holding a map now loads.
- **The "Random" map entry gave its map away.** The top screen showed the drawn map after Continue, and the question mark was drawn in the map's colours with a box around it. It is now a plain question mark everywhere until the battle starts.
- **Skip the intro screens** now covers the window with a short loading screen while the boot runs, instead of showing the logos racing past.

## New

- **Maps** is its own section of the settings menu, and the map browser is a catalogue: every map's picture with its name beneath, grouped by pack. Click a map to see its size, players, mines and ground, and to download it.
- **The game updates itself.** Settings > General > **Install update** downloads the newest release and installs it over this one, then **Restart now** starts it. **Install updates automatically** does this at startup by itself. Your settings, saves, ROM, mods and logs are never touched. This version is the last one to download by hand.
- **The AI uses Hotwire.** With **AI Uses Targeted Spells** on (Balance > Spells), the computer's heroes now also cast Hotwire on your siege units, and EMP.
- **AI Plays My Side** moved to Enhancements > Gameplay, beside the other computer-player options.

## Known limitations

- **The Top Screen panel** cannot be clicked yet (the minimap, the selected units, the build queue). This is being worked on for the next version.

## Files

- `LegoBattles-PC-0.3.1.zip`: the game.
- `LegoBattles-PC-0.3.1-runner-source.zip`: the corresponding source of the runner (GPL-3.0-or-later). The port's own source is this repository at this tag.
