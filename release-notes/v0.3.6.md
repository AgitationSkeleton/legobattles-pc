Version 0.3.6 of the LEGO Battles PC port: a Gold Rush target, saved teams for the third computer player, AI spell use, and a Balance fix.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **LEGO Gold Rush target** (Enhancements > Gameplay). Choose how many bricks an army needs to win a Gold Rush match, from 1,000 to 100,000; the game's own is 10,000. Online, the host's target applies (both players need this version).
- **Every army's bricks in Gold Rush.** "Show every army's bricks in Gold Rush" (on by default) puts a small panel at the top of the screen with each army's bricks against the target.
- **Saved teams for Computer Player 3** (Team Builder). With its army set to Custom, a new Team choice picks the Enemy Team (as before) or any of your saved teams, so the two computer opponents can field different custom armies.

## Changed

- **The AI casts its attack spells in a fight.** With "AI Uses Targeted Spells" on, a computer hero in combat used to pick its defensive buff over its attack spell almost every time. Attack spells now come first in a fight; the buffs are still cast out of combat.

## Fixed

- **Walk on water from the Balance menu.** Ticking Water for a land unit (Balance > Traits) left out the flag that lets it cross between land and water, so it could not cross on any map; imported balance packs were not affected. A unit you already set up on an older version keeps the old flag: untick and tick Water again to fix it.
