Version 0.4.1 of the LEGO Battles PC port: the level editor draws with the game's own art, Community levels becomes a catalogue, and two fixes.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Community levels catalogue** (Maps > Community levels). It works like the Map archive.
  - **Browse:** a grid of each level's minimap, drawn in the game's own colours.
  - **Find and sort:** search by level name or author, and sort by newest, oldest, most downloaded or most liked.
  - **Subscribe** to a level to add it to your mods/maps folder, then **Load new maps now** to play it. **Unsubscribe** removes it again.
  - **Like** the levels you enjoy. You get one like per level, and you can take it back.
- **Level Editor art.** Trees, buildings, units, heroes, mounts and golden bricks are drawn with the game's own sprites, in each player's team colour.
- **Level Editor palettes.** The terrain swatches and the map follow the level's world and palette. On Mars, for example, grass shows as the red ground. A custom palette or the FactionWars palette applies too.

## Fixed

- **Pause menu on wide windows.** When the battle view was as wide as the map, the pause menu's buttons slid off to one side of their labels, beside a stray light beam. This could happen on an ultrawide window, or after zooming far out before a match. The menu now stays centred.
- **Action Replay codes** now run exactly as they do in melonDS. Before, a code could behave differently from melonDS even in the same order and format. In particular:
  - a failed condition now skips everything up to its `D0` or `D2`, not just the next line;
  - `D2` no longer ends the rest of the code;
  - `D6`/`D7`/`D8` now advance the offset;
  - loops run the right number of times.

  The `D4` maths operations and `D9`–`DB`, `C5`, `C6` and `F` codes are now supported too. Codes still only touch the game's main memory.
