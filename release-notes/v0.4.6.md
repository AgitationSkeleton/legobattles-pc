Version 0.4.6 of the LEGO Battles PC port: easier custom units, a menu search, and computers that fight each other.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Search the menu** (Ctrl+F, the box at the top right). Type part of a setting's name or description; pick a result to open its page with the setting outlined.
- **Custom units, reworked** (Balance > Custom units):
  - All units are in a list on the left: your custom units at the top, every stock unit and building by role below, with a search box.
  - The unit you pick is on the right with three tabs: **Look** (the animated preview), **Stats** (change a custom unit's name, starting model or sprites, and every value; Save writes unit.json) and **Files** (which of its files the game will use, and what the last build did with each).
  - Siege units and transports now show as a turning 3D model in the team colour.
  - Units made the community's way work as they are: sheets beside unit.json, compressed NCBR files, and sheets named for the unit you drew (`hh_krazi_w0` for `hh_conq_w0`).
  - Frames left as exported no longer paint the starting unit back over your new sheets.
  - "asset" in unit.json picks another stock model or sprite set to start from (a transport on the Claw Tank's model).
  - A how-to guide: the ten steps in the page, and HOW-TO.txt in every unit folder.
  - Community units has its own page.
- **Computers Attack Each Other's Bases** (Team Builder, under Computer Player 3; off by default). With a second computer opponent that is not allied, each computer goes for the nearest base, not only yours.
- **Level Editor: Mirror (H) and Flip (V)** for a paste before you place it, or for a selection where it is.
- **Proper Creature Portraits** (Team Builder; on). The Wolf and the Shark show their heads on the top screen instead of a lantern and a sack.
- New settings for port changes that had none, all on as before: Team-Colour Unit Portraits, Custom and Randomizer Opponents, Mouse Wheel Zooms, Use Mod and Editor Maps, Lobby Shows the Current Map, Show the Lobby's Server.

## Fixed

- Team-coloured portraits went back to red and blue while a button was held, while paused, or under the victory banner.
- In DS Vanilla, the game's own drag-selection box was hidden with nothing drawn in its place.
- Large buildings no longer spill out of the custom units preview.
- Clicking a transport's empty occupant slot on the top screen is no longer offered; the tooltip says a click unloads that unit.
