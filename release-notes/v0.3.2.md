Version 0.3.2 of the LEGO Battles PC port: bug fixes from the Discord reports, a clickable top screen, and more for the neutral creatures.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. From 0.3.1 on, Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Fixed

- **Selecting a neutral creature on the army screen blacked out the screen and froze the game.** The ROM has no army-screen card for the Wolf; it now shows the game's "unknown" card.
- **Attack Boost did not reach ranged units.** The boost was worked out from a unit's melee damage, which archers, crossbowmen and most ranged heroes don't have, so they got nothing. A boosted shooter without melee damage now does double damage, as the boost does to a melee blow. The setting (Balance) is now simply Off or On: On boosts every shot that lands on its target, never the splash of a boulder, fireball or cannonball.
- **The blue checkmark for placing a building disappeared while the camera moved.** The original game hides the whole HUD while the camera scrolls. With WASD or edge scrolling that is most of the time, so the checkmark seemed to vanish. It now stays on the footprint while you scroll. A checkmark at the edge of the original screen area is also no longer cut by the footprint.

## New

- **The Top Screen panel can be clicked.** Click a unit's portrait to drop it from the selection, a queued unit to cancel it, and the minimap (click or drag) to move the camera. Clickable spots show a hand cursor and a tooltip.
- **Neutral creatures in your colours.** With Neutral Creatures in Armies on, an owned Wolf, Robot, Monkey or Shark is drawn in its owner's team colour, so you can tell whose it is. Wild ones look as before. Team Builder > **Tint creatures by team**.
- **Choose a creature's army slot.** Team Builder lets the Wolf, Robot and Monkey stand in Close Combat or Ranged, and the Shark in any of the three Specials. Online, the host's choice applies to everyone.
- **The Monkey** joins the Wolf, Robot and Shark as a neutral creature you can field.

## Custom maps

- **The Mars beta maps were redrawn**: their craters drew as flat brown strips. Download the pack again from the Maps page. Lobbies treat the new versions as different maps from the old ones.
