The first public test build of the LEGO Battles PC port. **This is a beta: please report what you find** on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legobattles-pc/issues/new/choose).

## Before you start

- **You need your own copy of the game.** The first start asks for your ROM (`.nds`), Europe or USA. A USA ROM is converted to the European image the port is built on, rebuilt from your own file; it is only read, never changed.
- Unzip anywhere and double-click `LegoBattles.exe`. F1 or Esc opens the settings panel.

## Known limitations

- **Online multiplayer is lightly tested.** Hosting, joining, and a full match have worked between copies on one network and through the public lobby server, but not yet across many different networks, routers or long matches. The host needs UDP port 26710 reachable from the internet. Desyncs, failed joins and lobby problems are especially useful to report -- please attach the logs from every player.
- **Tested on one PC so far** (Windows 10, one GPU). Other Windows versions, GPUs, drivers and monitor setups are untested; crashes at start-up, black or garbled pictures and frame-rate problems on your hardware are exactly what this beta is for.
- Windows only.

## Reporting

Attach the newest file in `logs\` beside the executable, and if you can the newest `lb-session-*.lbdemo` -- it replays your session exactly, which usually makes a bug reproducible here.

## Files

- `LegoBattles-PC-0.1.0-beta.1.zip` -- the game.
- `LegoBattles-PC-0.1.0-beta.1-runner-source.zip` -- the corresponding source of the runner the executable was built from (GPL-3.0-or-later), with the notices and licences. The port's own source is this repository at this tag.
