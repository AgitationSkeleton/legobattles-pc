Version 0.3.5 of the LEGO Battles PC port: fixes from the Discord reports, a steadier online relay, and an "All" score table.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Fixed

- **Online matches through the relay survive a short network drop.** A player whose connection dropped for a few seconds mid-match used to lose the match: the game froze, then said the connection was lost. The game now notices a dead relay connection within a few seconds and reconnects. The server keeps the other players waiting for the host for 15 seconds, and the match carries on where it was. Both players need this version.
- **Campaign music is the campaign's again.** The Music setting (for example Imperial) replaced a campaign level's own music. It now applies to Free Play and multiplayer only.
- **Bridges can be picked with a number key.** A builder with ships and specials has more than nine buildings, and the number keys only reached the first nine. **Shift+1-9** now pick items 10-18 of the open menu.
- **The third computer player on custom maps.** Some custom Free Play maps (Forest of Fate (Small), Ground Zero and others made with the map editor) never spawned the third player: the game read them as having too few starting bases. They now count correctly.
- **Smaller session logs.** Two diagnostic messages could fill a log with hundreds of megabytes over a long session. They are now limited.
- **The log says why the game closed.** If the game closes unexpectedly, the session log now records what asked it to close (the window, the menu's Quit, or the system), to help track down reports like "the game closed while I was changing the volume".

## Menu

- **Network > Scores > All:** every preset at once, one row per player and preset, with each preset in its own colour.
- **Team Builder** is now a menu category of its own, no longer under Enhancements.
- The menu's category row shrinks its text to fit smaller windows.

## Files

- `LegoBattles-PC-0.3.5.zip`: the game.
- `LegoBattles-PC-0.3.5-runner-source.zip`: the corresponding source of the runner (GPL-3.0-or-later). The port's own source is this repository at this tag.
