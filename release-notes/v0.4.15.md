Version 0.4.15 of the LEGO Battles PC port: 3D custom units are no longer black, units no longer borrow other units' sprites in three-army matches, plus new options for custom units and the computer players.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Fixes

- **Re-skinned 3D custom units were all black** (0.4.14). A model's texture has a separate set of colours for each team, plus the white hit flash. 0.4.14 wrote your texture over the first set only and blanked the rest. Every set is now filled. Your colours appear for every team, and the texture's three team blues still turn into each player's team colour.
  - Use at most 16 colours, and keep the exported texture's team blues where the team colour should show. The texture must be `model/texture.png`; a `.tga` is not read.
- **A unit drawn with another unit's sprites** (for example a Wolf showing Santa's frames, then buildings'), in matches with three armies. The game has room for a fixed number of unit sprite sets. A creature in the Mounted slot, or the same unit in two armies, could use them all up. The overflowing unit was then drawn from the first army's hero. When a match needs more than there is room for, armies now share the sprites they have in common.
- **Hearing other armies' upgrades far away.** The game plays the "upgrade complete" chime for every army's upgrades, wherever they are. A third computer player made it much more frequent. New option **Hear Only Your Own Upgrades** (Settings > Music, on).
- **Enemy ships, siege and special units showing through the fog.** The game draws one whenever any part of its model is near ground you can see. New option **Hide Enemy Specials in Fog** (Settings > Display, on): one is drawn only when the ground it stands on is in sight, as for every other unit.

## New

- **Custom palettes on the top-screen minimap.** With Level Palettes set to FactionWars or a palette of your own, the minimap now uses matching colours.
- **Custom units:**
  - **Trained at** (Stats tab, transports and specials): Automatic, Special Factory or Shipyard. It applies to your build menu and to the computer's.
  - **Damage readout** (Stats tab): damage per second, and how many hits and seconds it takes to beat a few stock units. It is an estimate; the tooltip says what it leaves out.
  - **Unlocked with:** a custom unit can share a stock unit's unlock. For example, it can be picked only once the Queen is unlocked on your save. The computer's armies are not limited.
- **The AI Builds Transports Only When Needed** (Enhancements > Gameplay, off by default). The computer no longer builds transports nobody asked for, which on land maps just sat idle and took the place of siege units. It still builds one when its troops need to cross water.
- **Session logs** now say what kind of match each one was: Free Play, online or story, and whether it was spectated. This helps with bug reports.

## Still open

- **Custom unit sprites can only use the game's unit colours** (12 fixed colours plus the team colour). Giving a custom unit colours of its own needs changes to how the game draws units; this is being looked into.
- **EMP on a hotwired vehicle froze a match** (reports from 0.4.8): not reproduced.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby should use 0.4.15.
