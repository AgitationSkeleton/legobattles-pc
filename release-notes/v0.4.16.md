Version 0.4.16 of the LEGO Battles PC port: big battles no longer slow the game down as much, units never borrow other units' sprites, and many new options for units, armies and maps.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## PC limits instead of DS limits

- **Big late-game battles run much closer to full speed.** Late in a large match, the DS's processor could not keep up, and the whole game ran at under half its normal speed (the frame rate stayed up, the game itself slowed down).
  - The busiest parts of the game's logic now run as PC code, with exactly the same results. One of them is finding which units are near a place, which alone took about a quarter of the DS's processor.
  - In a three-army match late in the game, the game now runs about 75% faster than in 0.4.15, still at 60 fps. It is not yet always at full speed; work on that continues.
  - New option **Native Game Logic** (Enhancements > Gameplay, on). Online, the host's choice is used. Replays keep the choice they were recorded with.
- **Units no longer borrow other units' sprites** in big matches. The DS has room for 33 unit sprite sheets; with three armies, creatures or custom units a match can need more. 0.4.15 made armies share sheets when room ran out; now every sheet simply gets a slot of its own. New option **Larger Sprite Pool** (Settings > Display, on).

## Fixes

- **The custom unit preview did not show your changes** (custom unit lab). It now shows your unit's own 3D texture, army-screen card, top-screen card and head, and updates as soon as you save a changed file. 3D units now show their cards and head too.
- Speed icon and transport choices set in Balance > Traits are now kept in replays.

## New

- **Damage against structures** (Balance > Traits, and the custom unit Stats tab): a unit's melee bonus or penalty against buildings, and a bonus or penalty for its projectiles (which had none).
- **Trained at** for stock transports and special units (Balance > Traits): Automatic, Special Factory or Shipyard.
- **Speed boost icon** per unit (Balance > Traits, and custom units): Automatic, legs or wheels.
- **Transport** per unit (Balance > Traits, and custom units): Automatic, can board, or cannot board a transport.
- **Creature unlocks** (Team Builder > Creature Unlocks): the Wolf, Robot, Shark and Monkey can each be tied to a stock unit's unlock.
- **Never in Random Armies** (Team Builder): units the Random army will not pick.
- **Unit bans by map type** (Balance > Ban Units, right-click a unit): ban it on all land maps, all water maps, or all large (128) maps.
- **Start Position** (Enhancements > Gameplay): where your army starts in Free Play.
- **Studs for Free Play Wins** (Enhancements > Gameplay, off by default): a few studs for each Free Play battle you win, not while the computer plays your side or with cheats on.
- **LEGO Store prices** per unit (Enhancements > Gameplay).
- **Random map** is now also on Enhancements > Gameplay (it was only under Ban Maps).
- **Session logs** now say which of the new options were on at the start of each match.

## Still open

- **Late-game speed** is better but not yet always full speed in the biggest battles.
- **The battle's group selection icon of custom units**: we have asked for details.
- **Custom unit sprites can only use the game's unit colours** (12 fixed colours plus the team colour).
- **EMP on a hotwired vehicle froze a match** (reports from 0.4.8): not reproduced.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby should use 0.4.16. Several new options change how a battle plays, and an older version would not follow the host's choices.
