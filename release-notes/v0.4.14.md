Version 0.4.14 of the LEGO Battles PC port: smoother big battles, xdelta patches for maps and custom units, smarter computer players, and fixes.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Faster

- **Big late-game battles run smoother.** Late in a large Free Play match (three armies, a large window), the game ran at 40 to 50 fps on a typical PC. Emulating the DS now costs about 20% less: about 11.7 ms per frame instead of 14.6 at a 980x680 view. In the same test, frames that took more than 20 ms dropped to about a quarter as many.
  - Nothing about the game changes: it plays out exactly as before, frame for frame.
  - An online match between two players stays in sync and runs at 60 fps on both.
- **Note:** in very large late battles the DS game itself runs out of processor time and slows down, as it would on a DS. That is a separate effect from the frame rate, and it is not changed in this version.

## New

- **xdelta patches for maps and custom units** (Maps > Patches).
  - **Export xdelta patch:** turns the original LEGO Battles ROM into one with your maps and custom units. Use "For this port" to share with other players of this port. Use "For a DS or another emulator" to put chosen maps into Free Play slots; custom units need this port and can't go into that kind.
  - **Import xdelta patch:** applies a patch to your ROM and installs the maps and custom units it contains, for example a map pack. A patch made for a different version of the ROM (USA instead of Europe) can be applied to that ROM instead.
  - Patches work with xdelta3, xdeltaUI and DeltaPatcher. Patches made with secondary compression can't be read; make them with `xdelta3 -S none`.
- **Computer player options** (Free Play, off by default):
  - **The AI Finishes Tower Upgrades:** the computer no longer restarts a tower upgrade that is already running. Before, long upgrades such as Tower III (especially with FunLite) never finished.
  - **The AI Keeps Its Bridges:** the computer no longer tears down its bridges.
  - **The AI Bridges When It Is Stuck:** the computer builds a bridge at the nearest bridge site when it can't reach its target, on more maps than before.
- **Upload dates** in the Community levels and Custom units browsers.
- **Level editor:**
  - Bridge sites can be placed without bridge posts or bank tiles: untick "Posts and bank tiles", or untick "posts" on a single site.
  - A start's units standing on water, mountains or trees is now a warning instead of an error, for puzzle maps and custom balance. A start's buildings there are still an error.
- **Showcase channel:** approved community levels and custom units are now also posted to the showcase channel on Discord.

## Fixes

- **Computer players trained land transports at the shipyard.** A custom land transport such as the Transport Tank is now trained at the special factory, as the player's is.
- **Re-skinned 3D custom units kept the stock look.** A custom special or transport's `model/texture.png` was ignored for every stock model. It now applies.
- **No hero could be picked after unsubscribing from a custom unit** that was in the saved army. Slots holding a custom unit that is no longer loaded go back to the King army's unit.
- **Custom heroes' names** now appear for computer opponents with a Custom army at the end of a match.
- **Bridge sites from other map editors** (such as LiruJ's map tool) are no longer refused. A crossing the game accepts is now a warning in the editor, not an error.
- **Long messages in the level editor's Checks** now wrap instead of being cut off.

## Still open

- **Enemy specials visible in explored fog** (Free Play, Enhanced): being investigated.
- **Hearing a third computer player's actions far off-screen:** the cause is narrowed down (some game sounds for that player have no position check) and is being worked on.
- **A creature's sprites cycling through other units' sprites:** we've asked for the armies used, to reproduce it.
- **EMP on a hotwired vehicle froze a match** (reports from 0.4.8): not reproduced.
