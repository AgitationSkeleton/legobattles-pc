The seventh test build of the LEGO Battles PC port. **This is an untested pre-release.** Each change was checked only in automated runs on one PC; none has been tried by players yet. Please report what you find in the Discord bug-report channel or on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legobattles-pc/issues/new/choose).

Everyone in an online match needs the same version.

## What's new (untested in real play)

### Every value these mods change can now be edited

The new **Balance > Traits** page adds the unit stats the Entities Editor edits that the menu was missing:

- **Land, water and trees:** where a unit can move. Land and Water also set the crossing and collision bytes the way the Entities Editor does.
- **Hitbox size**, and **Uninteractable**.
- **Projectile:** which projectile a unit fires, or Melee.
- **AI priority:** how much enemies prefer to attack it.
- **Defogged area:** how far a unit sees. It is also how far it attacks on its own, so raise it along with Range.
- **Speed "None"**, as some mods use for teleporting units.
- **Projectiles: Over trees.**

Melee damage and spread now go above 255.

### Import a mod's balance straight from its ROM

**How to use a mod's balance:**

1. Open the settings menu (F1 or Esc) and go to **Balance > Presets**.
2. Pick the mod in the preset list, if it is there.
3. If it is not there, press **Import from ROM…** and choose the mod's `.nds` file (or an `Entities.ebp`). Its balance is loaded into the menu as your own values, replacing any you had changed.

**How to share your balance with another player:**

1. In **Balance > Presets**, press **Export my balance (.json)…** and save the file.
2. Send the file to the other player.
3. They press **Import my balance (.json)…** and choose it.

### Renames

The new **Enhancements > Renames** page holds every cosmetic rename. All of them are off by default, they change only the text on your screen, and nothing goes online:

- Space Criminal Leader → Kranxx or Plisken (moved here)
- the Plantation's name (moved here)
- **new:** Claw Tank → Crystal Reaper
- **new:** Gemma → Doctor Kelvin
- **new:** Broadside → Governor Tyranny

### A notification sound

Incoming chat messages and other pop-up notices play a short sound from the game: by default "Group set". To change or turn it off:

1. Open **Settings > Music**.
2. Pick one from **Notification sound**, or Off. **Play** lets you hear it, and the **Volume** slider sets how loud it is.

### First-run setup asks for your save

After you choose your ROM, the setup now offers to import a save from a cartridge or an emulator. Press **Skip** if you have none. (You can still import later in **Settings > Saves**.)

### Other changes

- **Chat icons online.** The chat's hero icon showed nothing for a player using a Team Builder army. It now shows that army's hero.
- **Level Palettes.** The FactionWars choice is now named "FactionWars / FunLite2": FunLite2 uses the same palettes, except for its black King water, which this does not copy.
- **Clearer menus.** Several pages of the settings menu now say at the top what they do, and which settings affect online play.
- **README.** The README beside the game has a new **How to** section.
- **Corrected:** the README wrongly said the `mods` folder holds balance data. It holds maps only; for a mod's balance, use a preset or **Import from ROM…**.

## Known limitations

- **Testing so far.** Everything above has only had automated checks on one PC.
- **The Renames** were checked by their text scan, not yet seen on the game's screens.
- **The mount picture bug:** a mount or builder can show another unit's picture when the opponent's army includes it. It is known, visual only, and not fixed yet.
- **FunLite2's own maps and sprites** are not part of its preset: a preset is its balance only. Its maps can be used by putting its `.nds` in the `mods` folder.
