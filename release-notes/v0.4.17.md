Version 0.4.17 of the LEGO Battles PC port: big battles keep up at much higher speed, smoother play at large window sizes, team-coloured unit cards, and a round of fixes for custom units.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Speed

- **Faster CPU** (new option, Enhancements > Gameplay, on). In the biggest battles a DS's processor cannot keep up, and the whole game slows down even though the frame rate stays up. With Faster CPU the emulated processor speeds up only as far as a battle needs, up to four times a DS's, so the computer armies keep thinking at full speed. Menus and small battles run exactly as on a DS. Online, the host's choice is used; replays keep the choice they were recorded with.
  - Measured in a three-army Free Play late in the match at 3440x1440: the game now runs at full speed, where 0.4.16 ran at about three quarters of it, at about the same frame rate (high 40s to low 50s fps in the biggest battles).
- **Smoother at large window sizes.** The picture of each frame is now put together while the next frame is already being emulated, and the 3D scene is drawn in several bands at once, so big windows (up to 3440x1440 was tested) cost much less per frame.
  - Showing each frame at 3440x1440 now takes about a third of the time it took in 0.4.16.
- **Fixed: matches could freeze or crash** when the game switched between its internal tasks at an unlucky moment. This was rare at the DS's speed and common with a faster processor; it also showed up as units drawn as scattered streaks.

## New

- **Team-coloured unit cards** (Team Builder, off by default): the big picture of the selected unit or building on the top screen, and the victory and defeat picture, take its army's team colour instead of always showing the default one. Custom units can supply their own mask (`portraits/card_mask.png`, white = the parts that take the team colour).
- **Plays as** for custom units (Unit Lab > Stats): a custom close-combat, ranged or mounted unit, or a special, can count as another of those categories (on the army screen, for the computer and for build limits) while keeping its own sprites.
- **Which special** in the level editor: when placing a special unit, choose Special One, Two or Three.
- **Teal style "Red/Dark Blue (Unused)"** (Team Builder > Teal team colour): the game's other unused team colours, red with dark blue in place of grey.

## Fixes

- **A 3-player match with custom units and Random enemies could crash while loading.** The game ran out of sprite sheet slots; it now has spares.
- **The custom unit scroll bar** from the army screen no longer shows on the team colour screen.
- **Custom unit sprites with colours outside the game's palette** now load with the nearest colours (listed in Unit Lab > Files > Last build) instead of wrong ones.
- **Custom unit sounds**: 24-bit, 32-bit, floating-point and multichannel WAV files, files with extra data chunks, and upper-case `.WAV` names now load. A file that cannot be used is listed with the reason.
- **Custom 3D specials previewed in grey** when previewed as the Teal team.
- **The Unit Lab preview** now also shows the battle's group selection icon (in a match, custom units still show their template's icon).

## Still open

- **EMP on a hotwired vehicle** froze a match in online play (older reports). Not reproduced yet; if it happens to you, please send the session log.
- **Teal team specials drawn invisible** in some large matches: this version logs the cause if it happens; please send the session log.
- **Custom units' icons** in the battle's build menu and group selection still show their template's icon.
- **Speed in the very biggest battles** at very large window sizes is much better but not yet a perfectly steady 60 fps.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby should use 0.4.17: Faster CPU, Plays as and custom unit card masks change how a battle runs or which files a unit carries, and an older version would not follow them.
