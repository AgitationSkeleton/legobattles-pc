The eighth test build of the LEGO Battles PC port. **This is an untested pre-release.** It adds one feature to beta.7 and changes nothing else. Please report what you find in the Discord bug-report channel or on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legobattles-pc/issues/new/choose).

## What's new

### A picture of each online match in the lobby log

When you host an online match, your game draws one picture of it and posts it to the lobby servers' Discord log:

- each player's army: hero and units, with their icons and names;
- the map's Free Play preview and its name;
- the banned units, if there are any.

It is drawn by your own game from your own copy of the game, so no game files are sent anywhere: only the finished picture.

**To turn it off:** settings menu (F1 or Esc) > **Network > Multiplayer**, untick **Post a picture of my matches to the lobby log**. It is on by default.

## Known limitations

- **The map preview's colours** are close to the game's, but not exact.
- **Each player is drawn as their own team**, in a fixed colour by slot.
- **Three-player matches and mod maps** have not been tried with it yet.
- **Everything in beta.7's notes** still applies.
