Version 0.4.12 of the LEGO Battles PC port: the computer builds shipyards on any map.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **The AI Builds Shipyards On Any Map** (Enhancements, off by default; Free Play only). The computer builds a shipyard only at a map's own AI build sites. Seven Free Play maps with water have none, so armies whose special units are boats (Pirate and Imperial ships such as the Clipper, the Battleship or Brickbeard's Bounty) never fielded them there. With the setting on, such a map gets build sites beside its water. Tested on a lake map: with it on, the computer built a shipyard and then Battleships; with it off, none in six minutes. Maps that already have build sites are unchanged.

## Still open

- **EMP on a hotwired vehicle froze a match** (reports from one match on 0.4.8). It could not be reproduced, including with the stolen unit selected and its menus open when the EMP hit. The game now logs where a bad memory read comes from, so if it happens again, please post the session log from the logs folder.
