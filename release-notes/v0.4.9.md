Version 0.4.9 of the LEGO Battles PC port: a big batch from the bug and suggestion channels.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Custom units

- **Fixed:** custom unit names showed blank in game. Mod map names had the same problem.
- **Fixed:** mounted units' walk and attack play with their real 4 + 4 frames in the viewer and the export. Re-export a mount you made earlier: its frames moved.
- **Fixed:** custom unit sounds were far louder than the game's. They are now levelled, and **Custom Unit Sound Volume** (Settings > Music) adjusts them.
- **New: preview in every team colour, Teal included** (Look tab).
- **New: Credits.** A line in the Stats tab names who helped make the unit. It is shown under Community units and sent when you share.
- **New: Convert...** turns an .NCBR (with an .NCLR palette) into a PNG, and a PNG into an .NCBR.
- **New per-unit options**, in the Stats tab and in Balance > Traits for stock units:
  - **Mountains:** the unit can walk over mountains.
  - **Can't be Hotwired**, **EMP-proof** and **ESP-proof**.
  - **Construction style:** whether a builder's buildings go up as human, skeleton or alien construction.

## Level editor

- **Fixed:** Pirate and Imperial levels were labelled the wrong way round, and the Earth faction was called Space Police.
- **New: Patrol points.** Pick a campaign patrol path, drag its points, add a point with a click and remove one with Shift-click.
- **New:** red bricks, minikits and mission collectables show as their own icons instead of gold bricks.
- **New: Decorative tiles.** Paint the world's own decorative tiles (flowers, mountain peaks, the Mars patterns), with an eyedropper.
- **New:** removing a mine spot also removes its stone pad.
- **Changed:** starts separated only by water are a note, not an error. Players reach each other by transport.

## Computer players

- **Fixed:** with "AI Rebuilds Its Hero Every Time" on, a hero the computer could not build blocked its build list, and it never made another builder.
- **New: The AI Builds Bridges On Any Map** (Enhancements > Gameplay, off by default). The computer builds the map's bridges even when it doesn't need them to reach you.

## Controls and windows

- **Fixed:** holding Tab now fast-forwards an offline battle, not only the menus. The speed is 2x to 8x, set under "Hold Tab to fast-forward battles" (Settings > Controls). It is off online and while recording or replaying.
- **Fixed:** a quick click could swallow the building check mark. A click's release now always reaches the game before the next press.
- **Fixed:** the number-key shortcuts (buildings and spells) now work in DS Vanilla display too.
- **New: Top Screen in its own window** (Tools > Windows). The top screen gets its own window, which you can move anywhere, with the same clicks. Closing it puts the panel back.

## Online

- **New: Discord invites.** With Rich Presence on, a lobby with free seats can be joined from Discord: "Join" and "Ask to Join". It is on by default as "Discord invites to my lobby" (Settings > General).
- **Not yet fixed: EMP on a hotwired vehicle froze a match.** We could not reproduce it yet. This version logs exactly where such a freeze happens. If it happens to you, please post the session log from the logs folder.

## Files

- `LegoBattles-PC-0.4.9.zip`: the game.
- `LegoBattles-PC-0.4.9-runner-source.zip`: the corresponding source of the runner (GPL-3.0-or-later). The port's own source is this repository at this tag.
