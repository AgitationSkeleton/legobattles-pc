Version 0.3.0 of the LEGO Battles PC port. It adds score tables, a map browser, custom maps that fetch themselves in a lobby, sharper special units, AI vs AI, and more suggestions from the Discord. Please keep reporting in the Discord bug-report and suggestion channels or on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legobattles-pc/issues/new/choose).

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc.

## What's new

### Score tables (Network > Scores)

- **When a hosted online match ends**, its result goes to the lobby server's tables: who won, units and buildings destroyed and lost, and bricks collected. The numbers come from the game's own end-of-match statistics.
- **There is one table per balance ruleset** (Vanilla, the presets, Custom Rules). You can show only matches with or without unit bans, or only those where a particular unit was banned.
- **Each player's most-played hero** is shown beside their name.
- **What doesn't count:** matches with cheats on, matches shorter than a minute, and names the swear filter would change.
- **Keep me out of the score tables** (Network > Scores) leaves your matches out of the tables.

### Maps

- **Tools > Maps** is a map browser for the community map archive and this port's own new map repository. It shows each map's picture, size, setting, players, mines and ground. **Download** puts a map in your mods folder, and **Load new maps now** adds it to the game without a restart.
- **The port's own maps** ([legobattles-custom-maps](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legobattles-custom-maps)):
  - **Mars beta maps:** six cut maps rebuilt from their surviving pictures.
  - **Prototype Free Play maps:** 16 Free Play maps from the LEGO Castle and LEGO Pirates prototypes that differ from the final game's, each named "… (Proto)".
- **Joining a lobby on a custom map you don't have** now gets it for you. The game takes the community archive's copy when it is exactly the host's map, and otherwise receives the host's own copy. It then resets to the title screen with the map loaded, and you join again. The map is checked to be byte-for-byte the host's before it is used.
- **The "Random" map entry** now shows a question mark instead of the map it drew.

### The computer opponent

- **AI Plays My Side (Spectate)**: the computer plays your side too, so you can watch two AIs fight. The map is revealed while you watch. Offline only.
- **The Cheats Help** (Cheats page) offers me / me and the AI / the AI only. "The AI only" gives the red-brick cheats to the computer as a handicap for you. Offline only.

### Display

- **3D resolution** (Settings > Display; Native, 2x, 3x, 4x) draws the special units' 3D models (ships, siege and other specials) at a higher resolution, so their shapes and outlines are sharp in a large window. Everything else is drawn as before, and the models' textures stay as the game stores them. 2x keeps 60 fps.
- **Forced tilesets:**
  - Rock outcrops and plateaus no longer break into rectangles.
  - The ground around well-cap cracks no longer shows pieces of crack.
  - Tiles are now chosen by how well their edges join their neighbours.

### Gameplay options

- **Attack boost: shots only (no blast)** applies the attack boost to an arrow or bolt's hit but not to its blast. Online, the host's setting applies to everyone. The existing ranged option now also boosts arrows that land on their target.
- **Cancel a queued unit:** click its icon in the Top Screen panel's build queue (Enhanced mode). It runs the game's own Cancel and refund, so it is an ordinary game command online too. To turn it off, untick **Click a queued unit to cancel it** (Settings > Controls).
- **Neutral creatures in the army builder:** with the creature option on, the Wolf, Robot and Shark appear in the game's own army builder, with their heads.
- **Rebalance 1.18.1** is added to the balance presets, beside 1.15.2.

### Smaller changes

- **Balance > Units:** **Speed as stored value** shows and edits speed as the Entities Editor's number. Hovering any speed shows both.
- **Network > Multiplayer:** **Ping servers** times each lobby server.
- **Renames:** the Pirate prototypes' names. Shanty Shack becomes "Tavern" and Plantation becomes "Shanty Shack" (one option), and Brickbeard's Bounty becomes "Flying Dutchman".
- **Map-specific music** now plays either of the map's two factions, picked each match. For example, a Mars map plays Earth or Alien.
- **The lobby log** names banned units and maps instead of their file names.
- **Session recordings are off by default.** Turn on **Record every session** (Tools > Recordings) to record each session for bug reports.
- **Fixed:** reading from the lobby servers could wait several seconds after each reply.

## Known limitations

- **3D resolution** sharpens shapes, not textures, and has only been checked on a catapult and an airship.
- **The rebuilt beta maps** have invented start positions, mines and markers (each map's `.txt` says what). Most of the new maps have not been played through.
- **The Unit Heal spells** may still crash when cast. This is not reproduced yet.
- **Minikit specials under balance presets:** a report says their range stays vanilla. The preset's values are in the game, so a session recording would help.
- **The mount picture bug** is not fixed yet.
