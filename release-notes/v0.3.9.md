Version 0.3.9 of the LEGO Battles PC port: fixes to the brick drawers, the bridge preview and the teal colour swatches.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Fixed

- **The brick drawers' closing animation** now plays where the drawer is. As a drawer closed, its items were drawn lower on the screen than the drawer itself, in windows that show more than the DS's screen.
- **The name and price of a held item** (press and hold a building, unit or spell in a drawer) now appear beside the item, as on the DS, not at the top centre of the screen.
- **Bridge previews on long bridge sites**, such as the Mars map's: the green placement preview now covers the whole crossing. In a tall window it used to stop at the edge of the DS's own screen area.
- **Teal team colour:** when one player or computer has teal, the other's colour row now shows teal with that player's badge, like the other colours. It used to show a plain teal swatch.

## Files

- `LegoBattles-PC-0.3.9.zip`: the game.
- `LegoBattles-PC-0.3.9-runner-source.zip`: the corresponding source of the runner (GPL-3.0-or-later). The port's own source is this repository at this tag.
