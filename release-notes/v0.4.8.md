Version 0.4.8 of the LEGO Battles PC port: big custom maps fixed, online matches at the host's pace, and team colours in the match picture.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Online, everyone plays at the host's game pacing.** "Steady game pacing (30 Hz)" (Display) is the host's choice for the whole lobby and match.
  - Online, a match only moves as fast as its slowest player, so it used to take everyone turning the setting off to play at melonDS's faster pace.
  - While you are in someone's lobby, the setting shows which pace applies.
  - Both paces were tested holding steady in an online match.
- **Team-Colour Portraits in the Match Picture** (Online, under Share Match Telemetry; on). The match summary posted to the server's Discord shows each player's units in that player's team colour, by the same rules as Team-Colour Unit Portraits.

## Fixed

- **Custom maps taller than 96 tiles.**
  - The game keeps one row table for where units stand, and it only had room for 96 rows, the size of the largest stock map. On a bigger map the lower rows read garbage, so units got stuck on things that were not there.
  - The table now holds 128 rows, the level editor's largest.
  - Maps larger than 128 are left out, with a note in the mods report.
