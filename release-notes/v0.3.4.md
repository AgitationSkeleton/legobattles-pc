Version 0.3.4 of the LEGO Battles PC port: fixes from the Discord reports, reworked score tables, and more map sources.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## Fixed

- **A free 500 bricks from the hotkeys.** Pressing the hero's number key at a castle at the start of a match queued a hero that was never paid for, and cancelling it refunded 500. A number-key build now follows the same rules as tapping the button: what the button would refuse, the key refuses too.
- **The AI no longer spams EMP.** With AI Uses Targeted Spells on, a computer hero (Gemma, for one) cast EMP over and over, often at nothing, and stopped using its other spells. EMP and Hotwire are now cast only when an enemy siege or special unit is in reach, and never ahead of the hero's own boosts.
- **Spare projectiles kept a balance pack's own values.** In FUNLite, Rebalance and other packs, records 203-205 are the Air Fireball, Air Laser and Air Ballista Bolt. 0.3.3 overwrote them with the Arrow's numbers. A record a pack uses now keeps the pack's values and shows its real name; only unused records are offered as Spare projectiles.

## Score tables (Network > Scores)

- **One tab per balance preset:** Vanilla, Vanil+, FactionWars, FunLite, Rebalance, Custom Rules and others. A preset's versions are ranked together, with a Version choice to narrow it.
- **A clear choice of matches without or with unit bans.** With bans, **Choose units...** opens a picker of every unit with its icon: pick any number, apply a ban preset (such as the LEGO Battles Discord competitive bans), or use your own current ban list. **Exactly this ban list** shows only matches played under exactly those bans. A line above the table says what it shows.
- **Remove me from the scores table.** Removes your own results from the server. It works for matches recorded from this version on, which carry an ID unique to your copy of the game. You can also keep yourself out of future tables.

## Maps

- **lnee's maps** (codeberg.org/lnee/lbzmaps) are on the Maps page, listed first. They are `.lbz` files, the map tool's own packages, which the page now reads and installs as ordinary map folders.
- **Download all**, at the top of the page and for each pack.
- **Check the archives for new maps** refreshes the list from every archive. A newly added archive is now fetched even when the others are already downloaded.

## Other

- **The game has its own icon,** on the executable, the title bar and the taskbar.
