Version 0.4.25 of the LEGO Battles PC port: the Thunder Hammer fix now really works, the computer stops buffing your units, and a few smaller additions.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **The AI Builds Fewer Farms** (Enhancements > Gameplay, off by default, Free Play only). A computer with plenty of bricks used to keep two unit slots free, so it kept building farms, even past the unit cap where they add nothing. With this on it builds one farm at a time, and only when it has no free slot.
- **Uninstall maps from the maps archive** (Maps > Map archive). Maps you put in your mods folder yourself are left alone.
- **Fixed Top Screen Window Size** (Tools > Windows, off by default): presets from 1x to 4x the DS screen, or your own width and height, for the Top Screen in its own window.

## Fixes

- **The Thunder Hammer flew off diagonally down and right** when thrown at a unit. With **Fix Thunder Hammer** on (Balance > Spells), it now flies to the unit and explodes on it.
- **With "AI Uses Targeted Spells", the computer cast its unit buffs on your units.** Unit Speed, Unit Damage and Unit Armour Boost now always land on the computer's own side.
- **The Projectiles tab's "Used by" list stopped at four units.** It now shows four and "+N more", with the full list when you hover over it.

## Still open

- **Exporting spell and gold brick values into a ROM** and **AI awareness of gold bricks behind trees**: these need more research.
- **Story-only buildings as level-editor decor**: not yet.
- **Computer players in online matches**: held back; not ready yet.
- **Scripting and custom spells**: planned as a later project.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby should use the same version.
