Version 0.4.5 of the LEGO Battles PC port: team-coloured portraits, a smarter AI option, and a guide for custom units.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Team-coloured portraits.** The small round portraits of the selected units on the top screen now take their owner's team colour, the way the units' sprites do. Each head follows its own sprite: the aliens' bodies, Gripley's dome, the astronauts' torsos, the Ninja's cowl and so on; faces, hair and helmets keep their colours. It works for every team colour, teal included.
- **The AI Builds More Mines And Bridges** (Enhancements > Gameplay, next to the hero rebuild option; off by default). The computer keeps building mines until every free mine spot it can reach has one, instead of stopping at one, and builds up to three bridges. Free Play only.
- **A guide for custom units.** Balance > Custom units has a new **How to make a custom unit** section, ten steps with Back and Next. Every unit folder the game writes now has the same guide as HOW-TO.txt.

## Fixed

- **A custom unit ignored the model it named.** Setting `"asset"` in a custom unit's unit.json (for example a transport on `"Models/E_Clawtank"`) now uses that stock model, or a sprite set of the same role, instead of always the starting unit's.

## Files

- `LegoBattles-PC-0.4.5.zip`: the game.
- `LegoBattles-PC-0.4.5-runner-source.zip`: the corresponding source of the runner (GPL-3.0-or-later). The port's own source is this repository at this tag.
