Version 0.4.18 of the LEGO Battles PC port: custom team colours, spell editing in Balance, custom units' own battle icons and victory pictures, and a round of fixes from player reports.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Custom team colour** (Team Builder, off by default): pick any colour for your army with a colour picker, or a preset, including the game's own unused colours. Sprites, buildings, walls, 3D models, the minimap, portraits and cards all take it. Online, the host decides whether custom colours are allowed (off by default); colours too close to another player's are moved apart automatically. Replays show the colours they were recorded with.
- **Spell values** (Balance > Spell Values): edit each spell's damage at the start and end, hit chance, tap range, area, mana cost, duration and startup frames. They travel with the host's Balance online and are recorded in replays.
- **Soldiers Repair Buildings** (Balance, off): foot, ranged and mounted units can repair and help build, like builders. Select them together with a builder to give the order.
- **Collision layer** (Balance > Traits, and custom units): choose which layer a unit collides on (ground, air, bridge).
- **One Of Each Hero** (Balance > Unit Caps, off): with a raised hero cap, an army can't have two of the same hero at once.
- **Custom units' own icons in battle**: `hud_icon.png` is now used for the group selection, the training queue and the build lists, and a new optional `portraits/build_icon.png` for the build menu.
- **Custom heroes' victory and defeat pictures**: optional `portraits/end_victory.png` and `end_defeat.png` (256x192). Without them the template's pictures are used.
- **Remember Music And Effects Volume** (Sound, on): the game's own volumes now apply from the first screen, instead of the intro and title always playing at full volume.
- **Content packs** (Balance > Content packs): export chosen custom units, map folders, your Balance values and a preset of gameplay settings into one `.lbpack` file, and install someone else's. Nothing already installed is overwritten; Balance and settings are only applied if you tick them.
- **Custom units with a 3D model** (Unit Lab > Stats > 3D model): a custom hero, builder or ground unit can be drawn with a stock transport's or special's model while staying what it is (a hero keeps its spells and respawn). `model/texture.png` still recolours it, and a new optional `model/model.obj` replaces its mesh (the Files tab writes the stock mesh as `model_export.obj` to start from). A level has 12 model slots; when they are taken the unit is drawn with its sprites.

## Fixes

- **"libgcc_s_seh-1.dll was not found"**: the game no longer needs that DLL, or `libstdc++-6.dll` or `libwinpthread-1.dll`. Only `SDL2.dll` must sit beside `LegoBattles.exe`. If you still see a DLL error, extract the whole zip to a folder before starting the game.
- **Fast-forward stuck on**: releasing Tab while the menu was open, or while another window had focus, left the game fast-forwarding (about 150 fps) until Tab was pressed again. It now stops when Tab is up.
- **Steady game pacing off ran about 30% fast.** Off now plays the cartridge's own uneven steps at about the pace melonDS does (measured at a match's start: about 32 steps a second instead of 39). Older replays still play at the pace they were recorded with.
- **Team-coloured victory/defeat picture stayed stock** in Free Play (for example the Space Criminal Leader stayed orange): the result screen is now recognised.
- **Custom unit previews** now re-read every file when it changes, and "Reload art" reloads cards, icons, the 3D texture and sounds too.
- **Custom unit sounds** play in the Unit Lab preview (the "Own sounds" row) instead of the template's.
- **"Trained at: Shipyard"** for a custom unit now also decides the build menu bar, so a Shark-based unit with land movement is offered at the Shipyard, not the Special Factory.
- **AI Uses Targeted Spells** (which lets the computer cast Hotwire and EMP) is now recorded in replays, and its help says when the computer hotwires.
- Replays now record a changed Gold Brick value.

## Still open

- **Specials invisible when zoomed out** (Enhanced) and **phantom ties at the very start of an online match**: not reproduced here yet; we asked the reporters for details.
- **Bridge build previews at very large window sizes**: not reproduced yet.
- **EMP on a hotwired vehicle** froze an online match in older versions. Not reproduced; please send the session log if it happens.
- **A second hero slot**: planned, not built.
- **Custom units drawn with another unit's sprites** (for example a hero on a horse): written, but held back after it corrupted memory in a test.
- **Computer players in online matches**: planned for a later version.
- **Speed in the very biggest battles** at very large window sizes is still not a perfectly steady 60 fps.

## Please update together

- Online, everyone in a lobby should use 0.4.18: custom colours, spell values, the new rules and custom units' new files change what each machine needs, and an older version would not follow them.
