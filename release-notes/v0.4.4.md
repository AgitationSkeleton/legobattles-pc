Version 0.4.4 of the LEGO Battles PC port: player requests, rebindable shortcut keys, and a lobby fix.

The settings menu opens with F1 or Esc. Settings > General > **Install update** updates the game in place.

## New

- **Shortcut keys you can change** (Settings > Controls > Battle shortcuts > Shortcut keys). Items 1-9, Deselect, and two new keys: **Open blue menu** (B by default) and **Open red/yellow menu** (unset). Esc cancels a change and Delete clears a key. The keypad digits and the mouse buttons still work.
- **Move objects in the Level Editor.** The new **Move object** tool drags a building, unit, mine, AI marker, bridge site or golden brick to a new place. It also moves one piece of a starting base on its own: the base, barracks, farm, hero or a builder.
- **Hotwire Neutral Units In Missions** (under Hotwire targets; off by default). In single-player missions, Hotwire can take neutral and third-team units and buildings. It never applies online or in Free Play.
- **Stunned Heroes Can Cast** (Balance > Spells; off by default). A hero stunned by ESP or EMP can still cast its spells. Online, the host's setting applies.

## Fixed

- **The lobby showed the previous match's map.** From the second match on, the lobby could name the last map as the host's choice, even though the right map was played. The label now updates.

## Files

- `LegoBattles-PC-0.4.4.zip`: the game.
- `LegoBattles-PC-0.4.4-runner-source.zip`: the corresponding source of the runner (GPL-3.0-or-later). The port's own source is this repository at this tag.
