The third test build of the LEGO Battles PC port. **Still a beta: please report what you find** on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legobattles-pc/issues/new/choose).

## What changed

- **Online play without port forwarding.**
  - When you host, the game asks your router to forward its port (UPnP). This is on by default; the toggle is in the Multiplayer settings.
  - The lobby server also introduces the two players, so both routers let the game's traffic through even without UPnP.
  - The Multiplayer section shows your public address and whether anything has arrived from the other player.
- **A server list.** lbserver.viosarcade.xyz (the default), king.legobattles.win, or one of your own.
- **A Balance menu.**
  - Every unit's and building's values by faction (cost, build time, health, mana, speed, damage, attack interval and range, yield), and every attack's damage, editable in place, in a battle too (not online).
  - The balance packs are there now, with export and import of your values as a file.
  - Recordings carry the values.
- **Saves from anywhere.**
  - A save from a USA cartridge or an emulator imports and loads; padded emulator files work. Settings > Saves > Import restarts the game into it.
  - Your save is `saves\LEGO Battles.sav` whatever your ROM file is called.
  - The originals are kept as `.orig`.
- **The brick menus stay together.** A long list (a Pirate builder's buildings) or one sliding open no longer appears split or in the middle of the screen on wide windows.
- **The building preview is whole.** It no longer has a gap where the brick buttons sit on a DS screen.
- **New gameplay options, all off by default:**
  - the EMP disables transports too;
  - the AI gets the red-brick cheats you tick;
  - the AI rebuilds its hero every time it dies (the game stops after one rebuild);
  - a hotwired transport stops taking orders from its old owner.
- **The Cheats menu:**
  - Seven cheat names were on the wrong cheat; for example, ticking Upgraded Towers turned on Fast Harvesting. They are corrected.
  - Unlock All now also covers maps, concept art and every campaign mission (which also unlocks the cutscenes). It sets them where the game keeps them, and the game saves them.
- **Balance menu additions:**
  - A Heroes section to choose each hero's spells.
  - A projectile's speed and its on/off behaviours (piercing, flying over buildings, what stops it), with hit and impact damage separate.
  - The battle HUD's icons, with Tower II and III showing their upgrade icons.
- **Fixed: a freeze after hotwiring a loaded transport with Transport Occupants set to Destroy them.**
- **Recordings** now carry the cheats, so a replay plays the same game.

## Known limitations

- **Latency.** The DS wireless protocol waits for a reply on every exchange, so across the internet the connection's round-trip time limits the frame rate.
- **Routers that defeat both methods.** Two routers that both renumber ports for every destination (some carrier-grade NAT), with UPnP unavailable, still cannot connect. A relay is planned.
- **Testing so far.** On a single PC, and online between two copies on one network.
- **New options.** "Hotwired Transports Leave Their Old Owner" has not yet been seen at work in a battle; the other new options were each checked in one.

## Reporting

Attach the newest file in `logs\` beside the executable and, if you can, the newest `lb-session-*.lbdemo`.
