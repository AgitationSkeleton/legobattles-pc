The second test build of the LEGO Battles PC port, with fixes from the first online playtest. **Still a beta: please report what you find** on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legobattles-pc/issues/new/choose).

## What changed

- **Multiplayer runs much faster.** Two copies in a match went from about 30 to about 54 fps. The wireless bridge now waits on its network socket instead of sleeping between polls.
- **A new settings menu** in the style of Ship of Harkinian. F1 or Esc opens it. It has categories along the top, sections down the side, and Quit, Reset and Close buttons.
- **Reset** (or Ctrl+R) restarts the game from its title screen without closing the window.
- **Type on the game's keyboards.** On the profile-name and cheat-code screens, just type; Backspace erases and Enter is OK.
- **W/A/S/D move the camera** like the arrow keys. The DS X, Y and R buttons moved to C, V and E; all keys are rebindable.
- **The player name works.** With "Use the save's profile name" off, the typed name is what the other players see. A new button writes it into your save.
- **HUD buttons work fully zoomed out** on very wide windows. Before, a click there became a move order.
- **The window opens on your main monitor.**
- **The PC's date is used**, so the 12/25 Santa unlock can happen.
- **"Make All Builders The Same" picks the builder** (King, Wizard, Pirate, Imperial, Earth or Alien), not only the Imperial one.

## Known limitations

- **Online play across the internet is still untested and probably slow.** The DS wireless protocol waits for a reply on every exchange, so the connection's round-trip time limits the frame rate. A latency-tolerant layer is the next big piece of work.
- Tested on a single PC so far.
- Not yet fixed: the host's back arrow in the lobby sometimes does nothing until a player joins; the building-placement preview can split slightly; the move cursor can sit too low in the fog at some window sizes. Screenshots of these help.

## Reporting
