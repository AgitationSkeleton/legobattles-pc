The second test build of the LEGO Battles PC port, with fixes from the first online playtest. **Still a beta: please report what you find** on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/AgitationSkeleton/legobattles-pc/issues/new/choose).

## What changed

- **Multiplayer runs much faster.** Two copies in a match went from about 30 to about 54 fps. The wireless bridge now waits on its network socket instead of sleeping between polls.
- **A new settings menu** in the style of Ship of Harkinian. F1 or Esc opens it. It has categories along the top, sections down the side, and Quit, Reset and Close buttons.
- **Reset** (or Ctrl+R) restarts the game from its title screen without closing the window.
- **Type on the game's keyboards.** On the profile-name and cheat-code screens, just type; Backspace erases and Enter is OK.
- **W/A/S/D move the camera** like the arrow keys. The DS X, Y and R buttons moved to C, V and E; all keys are rebindable.
- **The player name works.** With "Use the save's profile name" off, the typed name is what the other players see. A new button renames your profile, and the game saves it the next time the main menu is up.
- **Number keys follow the blue menu.** While the blue brick's menu is open, 1-9 pick from it (Input settings; on by default). Otherwise they pick from the red, yellow or white brick's menu, as before.
- **HUD buttons work fully zoomed out** on very wide windows. Before, a click there became a move order.
- **The window opens on your main monitor.**
- **The PC's date is used**, so the 12/25 Santa unlock can happen.
- **"Make All Builders The Same" picks the builder** (King, Wizard, Pirate, Imperial, Earth or Alien), not only the Imperial one.
- **Menu buttons answer at every window size.** After a battle, some buttons on later screens could ignore clicks: the back arrow on the lobby and other screens, and the Free Play army builder's slots and rows. The battle HUD's click correction was still applying to screens that were not the battle.
- **The settings menu works in DS Vanilla.** It was drawn at twice its size and read the mouse at the wrong place, so clicks missed and Enhanced could not be turned back on.
- **With adaptive width off, the whole DS screen shows.** It used to be scaled to the window's width and cropped top and bottom.

## Known limitations

- **Online play across the internet does not connect yet unless the host forwards UDP port 26710** on their router. Without that, the router drops the joining player's packets. Connecting without port forwarding is the next piece of work. Even when connected, the DS wireless protocol waits for a reply on every exchange, so the connection's round-trip time limits the frame rate.
- **Saves from a USA cartridge show empty profiles.** The port expects the European save tag (`AFE`); a USA save carries `AFN`. A fix is planned; until then, `tools/lb_save.py import <save> <dest> --tag AFE` converts one.
- Tested on a single PC so far.
- Not yet fixed: the building-placement preview can split slightly; the move cursor can sit too low in the fog at some window sizes; fully zoomed out on a large window, the pause menu and quit dialog can draw cut off. Screenshots of these help.
